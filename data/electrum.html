Life at Electrum

Benefits at Electrum

Your own personal hardware

Every new starter gets a MacBook Pro and a killer workstation setup.

Life and illness cover

We’ve got you and your family covered with group life and critical illness insurance for total peace of mind.

Supporting your causes

From pet rescues to community projects, we’ll match your personal donations to help your favourite charities do more.

Socials with family and partners

Mid-year social including partners and end-of-year celebration with your immediate family.

Employee support programme

Access free, confidential coaching and counseling for everything from financial planning to mental wellbeing

Generous leave policy

20 days annual leave. Leave taken between 16 Dec – 1 Jan counts as half days.

Inhouse gaming

Table-tennis, foosball, arcade games, video games and board games.

On-site shower

Cycle or run into the office and make use of the company shower.

Catered lunch and a fully stocked kitchen

Free cooked lunch every day. Free snacks and drinks available in our fully stocked kitchen.

Café-quality coffee

Our cafe grade coffee machine and quality beans make the greatest espressos.

Friday socials

After a productive week, we unwind with beer keg, ciders, wine, craft beer, soft drinks and juice, available every Friday.
